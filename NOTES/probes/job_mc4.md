## The job
An MC4 solar connector pair hangs under a module: the female housing (the part your hand works on) is plugged into the male sleeve and latched. Unplug it and plug it back in, then let go.
The job is judged on these conditions, in this order:
- The hand holds the housing.
- The latch is released (its arms held open).
- The housing is pulled out until the plug's tip is at least 10 mm outside the sleeve.
- The housing is brought back in line with the sleeve, within 1.5 mm of its axis, and inserted.
- The latch engages again.
- The hand lets go and moves at least 50 mm away from the housing.
Damage fails the job whatever happens afterwards: pulling while the latch is engaged hard enough to break it, or pulling the cable beyond its tension limit.

## Why
The field moves weekly. A map of it that is a month old misleads whoever relies on it.

## What
- For each row of the watch list in `docs/field.md`: is there a new version, new weights, a new leaderboard entry, or new work that cites it and reports results on contact, force or damage?
- Write what changed as dated lines under "Changes" in that file, correct any statement there that is no longer true, and update "last checked".
- Add a row for anything new that bears on the question in the README ("The question being asked first"). Remove nothing; mark what is superseded.
- Paraphrase and link. Do not copy text or figures. Do not state a number you did not read in the source.
- In this item's log, add one line: the date and how many rows changed.
